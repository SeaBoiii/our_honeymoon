# Honeymoon Atlas

The written itinerary is the starting point. Every day has a stable anchor, a date, activities, food, an overnight stay and an entrance to the 3D player. Both players link back to the active day.

## Visual design

Use DM Serif Text for place names and DM Sans for practical information. The journal uses warm paper, forest green and lake blue, with a dark palette for devices set to dark mode. The original atlas cover and five winter landscape illustrations share Blender models with the map.

## Patterns

- Use the desktop day index and the native mobile picker to navigate the same fifteen chapters.
- Give Lake Tekapo, the Wānaka tree, Milford Sound, the Shotover Onsen and Franz Josef illustrated headers. Use compact text headers for the other days.
- Link activities to their own 3D scenes. Route pins lead to a day or offer the dates that share a stop.
- Keep Play, progress, Days and More within reach on phones. Put secondary controls in More.
- Place transport details in the chapter footer and the full driving edition in the map section.
- Use a table for desktop comparisons and labelled, stacked entries on phones. Keep the existing food status labels and browser checklist.
