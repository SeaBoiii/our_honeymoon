Is this the best honeymoon route?

Yes. Keep the route and all 13 nights. Codex compared every stop with the classic alternatives: Pukaki/Twizel stargazing cabins, The Hermitage, a second Wānaka night, a Milford overnight cruise, Glenorchy lodges, Hokitika, Kaikōura, Hanmer Springs and an Akaroa overnight. This loop has the best late-winter scenery and still leaves room to retry Milford and the heli-hike if the weather turns. The one weak spot is four one-night stops at the start. A second night in Wānaka is only worth it if we drop Akaroa.

Booking plan

Book in this order

  1. Scarce rooms: Aoraki Court Spa Studio, the Rainforest Spa Tree House, and the Queenstown lake-view suite
  2. Heli-hike (first departure on 4 Sep), Milford coach and cruise, Dark Sky tour
  3. Onsen sunset slot, then the ski lesson package
  4. Everything else, with Akaroa last

Aim for free cancellation up to 48–72 h before arrival. A weather refund on an activity doesn't cancel the hotel, so ask both.

Ask every hotel

  • Kitchen: does this exact room have a working hob and pans?
  • Halal: can you prepare food without pork or alcohol, using separate utensils?
  • Prayer: can you provide a prayer mat and the qibla direction?
  • Spa: is the tub/pool private to us for the whole stay?
  • Extras: can you replace any wine or champagne with something alcohol-free?
