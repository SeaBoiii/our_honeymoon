# Our New Zealand Honeymoon — South Island Loop
**24 Aug – 7 Sep 2027 · 13 nights in NZ · Christchurch round trip · self-drive**

Route and activity research and the hotel shortlist are by Codex (gpt-6.1-sol, high effort, Nov 2026). The full sourced research reports and flight booking details are kept offline.

> **Is this the best honeymoon route?** Codex reviewed every stop against the alternatives and said keep it as it is. The alternatives it weighed were Pukaki/Twizel instead of Tekapo, The Hermitage, a second Wānaka night, a Milford overnight cruise, Glenorchy lodges, Hokitika, Kaikōura, Hanmer and an Akaroa overnight.
> - **Why it works:** it balances the best late-winter scenery with weather buffers for Milford and the heli-hike.
> - **Weakest point:** four one-night stops in a row at the start. A second Wānaka night would only be worth it if you drop Akaroa.

> **Food key:** ✅ FIANZ-certified halal · 🟡 Muslim-owned or says halal, but not FIANZ-verified, so ask · 🍳 cook in your room
> **Stays:** the main pick is the one Codex recommends. Every stay from Tekapo onwards has a kitchen or kitchenette, because halal dining is rare outside Christchurch and Queenstown. Prices are Codex's **planning estimates in NZ$ per night**, not quotes.

## Route at a glance

Christchurch (1) → Lake Tekapo (1) → Aoraki/Mt Cook (1) → Wānaka (1) → Te Anau (2) → Queenstown (3) → Franz Josef (2) → Christchurch (1) → Airport (1)

## Day by day

### Day 0 · Tue 24 Aug — Singapore → Christchurch
- **Things to do:** SQ297 from Changi T3 at 19:50. It's an overnight flight.
- **Food:** pre-order the **Muslim meal (MOML)** for both flights in Manage Booking.
- **Night:** on the plane.

### Day 1 · Wed 25 Aug — Arrive Christchurch
- **Things to do:**
  - Land 09:30. Pick up the rental car, check the snow chains, and ask staff to show you how to fit them.
  - Dhuhr or Asr at **Al Noor Mosque**, 101 Deans Ave.
  - Walk the Botanic Gardens and along the Avon. Daffodils should be out.
- **Food options:**
  - ✅ **Chicking Bush Inn**, 364 Riccarton Rd.
  - 🟡 **Dubba Dubba**, 11B Waimairi Rd.
  - Groceries: **PAK'nSAVE Riccarton** for rice, eggs, snacks and a cooler bag, and **Maihan Halal Meat**, 403 Colombo St, for 3–4 days of meat.
- **Where we stay:** **Argyle on the Park**, 145 Deans Ave, ~$170–240.
  - Next door to the mosque and Hagley Park.
  - Every unit has a kitchen.
  - *Nicer alternative:* Hotel Montreal Junior Suite, ~$320–480.

### Day 2 · Thu 26 Aug — Lake Tekapo (~3–3½ h drive, 227 km)
- **Things to do:**
  - Drive across the plains via Geraldine and Fairlie.
  - Lakefront walk and the Church of the Good Shepherd.
  - Afternoon at **Tekapo Springs** hot pools.
  - **Dark Sky Project** Summit Experience on Mt John after dark.
- **Food options:** 🍳 cook in the suite. Four Square Tekapo, 22 Rapuwai Lane, for top-ups.
- **Where we stay:** **Peppers Bluewater, One Bedroom Lake View Suite**, ~$330–500.
  - Lake-view balcony, bathtub, and a full kitchen with a gas hob.
  - *Splurge:* **SkyScape SkyBed** near Twizel, ~$700–1,100. Glass roof over the bed and a private outdoor bath. It's 50 minutes from Tekapo's evening activities, and you pay in full if you cancel within 30 days.
  - *Best value romantic:* Galaxy Boutique Hotel, ~$350–500. Skylight over the bed, but no kitchen.

### Day 3 · Fri 27 Aug — Aoraki/Mt Cook (~1½ h + Twizel stop)
- **Things to do:**
  - Groceries in Twizel first: **Four Square Twizel**, 20 Market Pl. There's no shop in Mt Cook village.
  - Drive the Lake Pukaki road.
  - **Hooker Valley Track**: 10 km, so allow 4–5 h in winter. Check DOC conditions first, and bring microspikes and poles.
- **Food options:** 🍳 cook. Nothing in Mt Cook village is verified halal.
- **Where we stay:** **Aoraki Court, Executive Spa Studio**, ~$380–550.
  - Views toward Mt Sefton, an indoor spa bath, and a kitchenette with a hob.
  - *Splurge:* The Hermitage Premium Plus, ~$500–800. The best Aoraki views, but **no food-heating facilities** in the rooms.
- *Jumu'ah:* no mosque nearby. Pray in your room, or combine as travellers.

### Day 4 · Sat 28 Aug — Wānaka (~3 h via Lindis Pass, 209 km)
- **Things to do:**
  - Cross **Lindis Pass** in daylight after checking the NZTA road report.
  - Lakefront walk, and **That Wānaka Tree** at sunset.
- **Food options:**
  - 🍳 cook.
  - 🟡 **Bombay Palace**, 145 Ardmore St. They say they use halal meat, but they also serve alcohol.
  - Groceries: **New World Three Parks**.
- **Where we stay:** **Edgewater, Premium One Bedroom Suite**, ~$350–550.
  - Full kitchen with an oven.
  - **Get the lake view confirmed in writing.**
  - *Splurge:* **Lakeside Apartments Penthouse**, ~$1,000–1,400. Private outdoor spa pool, a two-person spa bath and a full kitchen.

### Day 5 · Sun 29 Aug — Te Anau (~3½–4 h via Cromwell, 274 km)
- **Things to do:**
  - Go via **Cromwell**, not the Crown Range.
  - **Big shop at PAK'nSAVE Frankton** for Te Anau and the Milford picnic.
  - Late afternoon: **Te Anau Glowworm Caves** boat trip.
- **Food options:** 🍳 cook. Nowhere halal is verified in Te Anau. FreshChoice, 5 Milford Cres, for fresh food.
- **Where we stay:** **Radfords on the Lake, lake-view One Bedroom Suite**, 2 nights, ~$320–460 a night.
  - Lake outlook and a kitchenette.
  - Free cancellation up to 48 h before arrival.
  - ⚠️ *Fiordland Lodge* had a fire in Feb 2026 and its reopening is unconfirmed. Don't rely on it.

### Day 6 · Mon 30 Aug — Milford Sound
- **Things to do:**
  - **Coach and cruise from Te Anau** with RealNZ: Eglinton Valley, the Homer Tunnel, Mitre Peak and the waterfalls.
  - DOC recommends the coach in winter.
- **Food options:** 🍳 pack a halal lunch, snacks and water. Have an easy dinner back at Radfords.
- **Where we stay:** Radfords on the Lake, second night.

### Day 7 · Tue 31 Aug — Queenstown (~2 h, 172 km)
- **Things to do:**
  - Slow morning on the Te Anau lakefront.
  - If Day 6 was cancelled and the road reopens, retry Milford today.
  - Drive to Queenstown and walk the lakefront.
- **Food options:**
  - 🟡 **Erik's Fish & Chips**, 13 Earl St. They say they're certified, so ask to see it.
  - 🟡 **The Taj**, 75 Beach St.
  - 🟡 **Bombay Palace**, 66 Shotover St.
- **Where we stay:** **Villa del Lago, One Bedroom Corner Suite**, 3 nights, ~$450–650 a night.
  - Floor-to-ceiling lake views, a gas fireplace, a full kitchen and laundry.
  - Codex's best overall Queenstown fit.
  - *Splurge:* **Azur Lodge** private villa, ~$1,500–2,400, with a bath in the bay window. Or **Eichardt's Earl St apartment**, ~$2,000–3,200, which has a kitchen.

### Day 8 · Wed 1 Sep — Snow day and Onsen
- **Things to do:**
  - Beginner ski lesson at **Coronet Peak**. Its 2027 season runs 18 Jun – 3 Oct.
  - At sunset, the private cedar tub at **Onsen Hot Pools** above the Shotover.
  - Tonight is the new moon.
- **Food options:**
  - 🟡 **Pedro's House of Lamb**, 47 Gorge Rd, as takeaway.
  - Or 🍳 cook at the villa.
- **Where we stay:** Villa del Lago.

### Day 9 · Thu 2 Sep — Glenorchy
- **Things to do:**
  - The lake road to **Glenorchy** and the lagoon boardwalk, with a picnic.
  - If it's wet, go to Arrowtown instead.
  - Visit **Queenstown Islamic Centre**, 159 Gorge Rd.
- **Food options:** 🍳 picnic and cook. Stock up for the West Coast at PAK'nSAVE or New World Frankton.
- **Where we stay:** Villa del Lago.

### Day 10 · Fri 3 Sep — Haast Pass to Franz Josef (~6–7 h, 404 km)
- **Things to do:** Lakes Wānaka and Hāwea, the **Blue Pools**, **Haast Pass**, then up the wild West Coast. Leave early with a full tank.
- **Food options:** 🍳 pack lunch. Four Square Franz Josef, 24 Main Rd, for top-ups.
- **Where we stay:** **Rainforest Retreat, One Bedroom Deluxe Spa Tree House**, 2 nights, ~$600–900 a night.
  - A raised rainforest tree house with a **private outdoor spa pool** and a kitchen.
  - *Value:* Glenfern Villas, ~$250–380, with a full kitchen. That saves about $700–1,040 over the two nights.
- *Jumu'ah:* driving day. Pray as travellers.

### Day 11 · Sat 4 Sep — Glacier heli-hike
- **Things to do:**
  - Book the **first heli-hike departure**. It needs a four-hour window of good weather.
  - If it's cancelled, try again on the morning of Day 12.
  - Glacier Valley walk in the afternoon, then the spa pool.
- **Food options:** 🍳 cook.
- **Where we stay:** Rainforest Retreat.

### Day 12 · Sun 5 Sep — Arthur's Pass to Christchurch (~5½–6½ h, 376 km)
- **Things to do:** Hokitika Gorge or the beach, then **Arthur's Pass**. Try to arrive before dark, around 18:05.
- **Food options:**
  - ✅ **Chicking Colombo**, 303 Colombo St.
  - 🟡 **AFG Restaurant**, 316 Lincoln Rd.
- **Where we stay:** **Hotel Montreal, Junior Suite**, ~$320–480.
  - A boutique city finish with a balcony and kitchenette.
  - *Splurge:* The George Park Suite, ~$650–950. It has a published Romance Package; ask for an alcohol-free version.

### Day 13 · Mon 6 Sep — Akaroa
- **Things to do:**
  - Day trip to **Akaroa** (~1½ h each way) for the Black Cat harbour cruise and a chance to see Hector's dolphins.
  - If you're tired, do Hagley Park and Al Noor instead.
- **Food options:** farewell dinner at your favourite Christchurch halal place.
- **Where we stay:** **Novotel Christchurch Airport, Superior King**, ~$250–360.
  - Have dinner before checking in.
  - *Upgrade:* the King Suite, ~$400–580.

### Day 14 · Tue 7 Sep — Fly home
- **Things to do:** be at the airport by 07:50 for **SQ298 at 10:50**. You land in Singapore at 17:40.
- **Food options:** the MOML meal on board.
- **Prayer:** the airport prayer room is landside, beside domestic baggage claim.

## Book in this order
1. The scarce rooms: **Aoraki Court Spa Studio**, the **Rainforest Spa Tree House** and the **Queenstown lake-view suite**.
2. The **heli-hike** (first departure on 4 Sep), the **Milford coach and cruise**, and the **Dark Sky** tour.
3. The **Onsen** sunset slot, then the ski lesson package.
4. Everything else. Leave Akaroa until last.

Aim for **free cancellation up to 48–72 h before arrival**. A weather refund on an activity doesn't cancel the hotel, so ask both.

## What to ask each hotel
- **Kitchen:** does this exact room have a working hob? Which pans and utensils does it have?
- **Halal meals:** can you prepare food without pork or alcohol, using separate utensils? Please confirm in writing.
- **Prayer:** can you provide a prayer mat and the qibla direction?
- **Spa pool:** is the tub/pool private to our unit for the whole stay?
- **Alcohol-free honeymoon extras:** can you replace any welcome wine or champagne with something alcohol-free?
